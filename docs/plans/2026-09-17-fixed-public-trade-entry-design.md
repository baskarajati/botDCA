Use the first archived public trade at or after the exported entry timestamp,
never a hindsight-selected low, high, or private exported average. Freeze the
training-only regime TP and current weighted-average DCA ladder. Retain both
one-second OHLC paths and the existing 300-second close tolerance.

Report close-only matches separately from joint close-and-depth matches. Run
previously successful cycles too, so regressions cannot be hidden. Market
prints are not proof of executable private fills. Sparse trade seconds and
intrasecond ordering remain limits; timestamp coverage does not prove the
exchange archive is complete. Entry timestamps are supplied by the CSV, not
generated by the strategy. This is an anchored diagnostic, not untouched
validation, autonomous signal reproduction, profitability, or deployment proof.

Keep all downloaded archives and private-source reports outside the repository.

runtime defaults. Investigating residual discrepancies does not authorize
promoting fitted rules to runtime.
